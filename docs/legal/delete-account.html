Delete your GymLog account

Deleting your account is permanent. Your sign-in, profile, and all synced data are removed. This cannot be undone. You never need to sign in on this page.

The fastest way: inside the app

  1. Open GymLog and go to Profile.
  2. Open Settings (top right).
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Takes under a minute. Your cloud data, profile, and on-device history are removed immediately.

What will be permanently deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile on our servers.
  • Any workout, routine, or preference data synced to the cloud.
  • All workout history, routines, and custom exercises stored on your device.

What stays yours

  • CSV files you exported to your device are your property and are never touched.

Don't have the app anymore?

Message us in the GymLog Telegram channel from your registered email address and we will delete your account within 30 days. Include the email you signed up with so we can find the account.

GymLog is local-first. If you only used the app without signing in, uninstalling it removes everything already.
