### Findings, regressions, and smallest improvements

No blocking or major defect was established. Two minor costs are visible:

1. **The Home-choice promotion duplicates the first routine and reduces library scanning density.** In the chosen 1.6× before render, Push A and Pull A are fully visible and Legs A begins above navigation. In the chosen 1.6× after render, the hero and its repeated Push A consume that space. The below-fold render confirms standalone rows still exist; they must not be described as lost. The smallest optional improvement is to condense the compact Home-choice card or its status line, retaining a clear named Start/Resume and truthful age.
2. **Home-choice recovery has a detour in the library.** `library-no-choice-blazeOrange-1.0x-synthetic.png` and its exact card crop show “Choose on Home.” as an instruction, not an inline chooser. Source `821–825` and `1020–1039` confirms that presentation. This concerns an optional Home default; the rendered saved routine Starts still permit the primary library task. It is therefore a minor convenience issue, not a blocked workout or regression of a former library chooser. If a direct route is desired, the smallest change is a clearly labeled **Go to Home** action that preserves the Home-owned chooser policy.

The removed global “Last trained” subtitle reduces overview recency, but chosen and routine-specific recency remains present. This is a small information tradeoff. The library's loading render shows both a named launchpad loading state and list skeletons; their different purposes are understandable, though the presentation could be quieter.
