<objective>
Reroute the routine-detail muscle load bar (`MuscleLoadBar`) from its hardcoded
static purple ramp to the user's selected settings accent.

Purpose: `lib/shared/widgets/body/muscle_load_bar.dart` is the last live surface
still painting `AppColors.muscleSplitPalette` (6-step `0xFF7F00FF`-based ramp).
The sibling widget `MuscleSplitSection` was already migrated to the reactive
ramp, and the entire ThemeExtension infrastructure is complete and registered —
so this fix is a 2-line change (swap the palette constant for a
`context.accent.muscleSplitRamp` read + one import). Corrections are test-first,
with a new widget test (RED) and a 6-accent golden baseline, then the
verify.ps1 migrated-screen gate is hardened with this surface.

Output:
- Fixed `muscle_load_bar.dart` plus the accent ramp instead of the static constant
- New passing `test/muscle_load_bar_accent_test.dart` + `test/golden/muscle_load_bar_golden_test.dart` with baselines in both `windows/` and `ci/` golden folders
- `scripts/verify.ps1` gate extended to cover the surface
</objective>
